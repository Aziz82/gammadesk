GammaDesk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

GammaDesk ("the app", "we", "us") is a free options-analysis tool for iOS, iPadOS and Android. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: GammaDesk has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and it does not collect personal data.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data. There is no account, email or sign-in.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell data or use it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Your settings stay on your device.

1. Information the app handles

Market data requests. To show market analytics, the app requests options and price data from the GammaDesk data server, and in some cases delayed public data from Cboe. These requests contain only the market symbol being viewed. They carry no account, name, email or device identifier. As with any internet service, the servers receiving a request see your device's IP address. The GammaDesk server uses it only to deliver the response and to protect the service from abuse, and does not use it to identify you or build a profile.

App settings. Preferences such as the selected symbol, alert thresholds and language are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us.

Notifications. Alerts are generated on your device. GammaDesk does not use a push notification server.

No trading or financial-account data. GammaDesk does not connect to brokerages, place trades, move money, or record positions, orders or profit and loss.

2. Third parties

  • Cboe may receive anonymous requests for delayed public market data, under its own terms.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores, under their own privacy policies.

The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics or sign-in SDKs.

3. Data retention and deletion

Settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Since we hold no account or personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

4. Security

Network requests use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

5. Children

GammaDesk is not directed to children and is intended for adults making their own analysis decisions.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact

GammaDesk is a personal analysis tool. Market data may be delayed or estimated, sourced from third-party providers, and may be inaccurate or unavailable. Dealer positioning values are model estimates, not measurements. Not investment advice.
