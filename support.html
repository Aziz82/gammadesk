Common questions

The data screens are empty or show an error. GammaDesk needs a market-data source. In Settings → Data, choose a provider and enter your API key or sign in (Polygon.io, Tradier, Schwab, or tastytrade). Data also updates only during U.S. market hours.

How do I delete my account? Open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its associated data.

Is this trading or investment advice? No. GammaDesk is an analysis tool only. It does not place trades and never tracks profit or loss. Dealer-positioning values are model estimates, not measurements.
